Clash Mini offline

Version 1.1143.2, Clashmas 2021, with its own server inside the game

This game runs entirely on your phone. There is no Supercell server, no support chat and nothing to buy with real money. Your progress is stored on this device only.

A snapshot of Clashmas 2021

  • The game stays as it was at the end of December 2021: Season 1 "The Adventure Begins", Clashmas in the shop, the Christmas look.
  • Days still go by: daily quests and shop offers are new every day.
  • Season 1 starts over every two months, so the Adventure Road stays worth playing.

Differences from the original

  • Free Mini Pass: the pass lane of the Adventure Road is open to everyone.
  • No real-money offers: no gem packs; the Clashmas Skins bundle is free.
  • Bots instead of players: you play Duels and Rumbles against the game's own bots, with their own decks for your league.
  • Where the original had several variants, the one that is best for you and really existed is used.

Tips

  • Every daily quest gives Pony Fuel for the Adventure Road.
  • Six daily quests a day, all shown at once.
  • You can reroll one quest a day.
  • The shop has three minis for gold and three heroes for gems every day.

New

  • New players get the tutorial quests (change the deck, play with Wizard, upgrade a hero, play a ranked match). Everyone gets the competitive quest "Win 5 rounds in Duel or Rumble" for a Crystal Coin, again and again, and "Use 100 Crystal Coins" can be a daily quest.
  • Two more Clashmas quests: "Win 10 trophies" and "Use Prince to Charge 3 enemies in a single match". "Win N Trophies" is a daily quest again.
  • Season end as in the original: the "Season has ended!" popup, then the star chest (with the Adventure Road rewards you did not claim).
  • A newly unlocked mini's quests show at once.
  • The season's end, every two months: road rewards you did not claim come by themselves, your minis' stars are exchanged for rewards (gold, gems, Crystal Coins, fragments; the minis go back to level 1 and lose their fragments, as in the original), and trophies from Diamond 3 up go back. The "Season has ended!" popup shows your stars, as in the original.
  • Skins in the shop: Clashmas Special (Jolly Barbarian King 159 gems, Helpful Mini P.E.K.K.A 99 gems), the Clashmas Skins bundle (Gift, Sparkle, Snowler) for free, and for Crystal Coins the Crystal P.E.K.K.A (100) and the Fabulous Prince (600).
  • Hero quests in the daily quests: win rounds or play matches using the day's hero, for gold, Pony Fuel and hero fragments.
  • Mini quests come with a mini you newly unlock, as in the original: win 1, 3 and 5 matches using it.
  • Skins as in the original: everyone starts with the default skins. Unlock them on the Adventure Road (P.L.U.S.H. P.E.K.K.A, Luchador Monk), with the Clashmas quests (Candy Elf) and in the shop; a skin you got stays yours. The four older skins (Celtic Magic Archer, Scientist Electro Wizard, Gravedigger Miner, Victory Shield Maiden) are everyone's.
  • Clashmas quests: three at a time; finish 12 to unlock the Candy Elf skin.
  • More daily quest kinds, counted in battle: win rounds, knock out enemies, win without rerolling, 3:0 in a duel, rounds without losing a mini, Clash abilities, upgrades in battle.
  • Crystal Coins stay: fragments beyond a unit's max level become one Crystal Coin each, also after a restart.
  • Boards by league as in Season 1: from Silver on you play on Electro Valley and Healing Temple, from Gold on also on Banquet Hall.
  • A help page instead of the support chat (this page).
  • Shop with the original prices: minis 250 / 500 / 750 gold, 25 hero fragments for 100 gems.
  • Six daily quests at once with the original rewards (Pony Fuel and gold).
  • Adventure Road with 90 tiers as in Season 1, free Mini Pass.
  • Bots with their own deck levels, as in the original.
