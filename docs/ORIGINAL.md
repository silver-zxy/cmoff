# The original game around 1.1143.2 (research)

What the live game was like in the time of our client, from public
sources. It guides what the server rebuilds; the client's own data and
code stay the authority where they say something.

## Our version

1.1143.2 is the Clashmas update of 21 December 2021 (soft launch):
Royal Champion, Musketeer, Prince; Clashmas skins; three deck slots; Mini
Rooms (friend battles by code, not counted for quests or ranked);
Challenge Coins removed (ranked unlimited) and Crystal Coins added. [1][2][3]

## Economy and progression

- **Crystal Coins** buy special skins in the shop. They come from the
  fragments a hero or mini gets after its max level or star level. [2][3]
- **Daily quests** renew every 24 hours, more varied with the Clashmas
  update; a larger pool of hero quests. **Mini quests** unlock with a new
  unit and ask to win with it. [3][4] Later sources speak of up to six daily
  quests a day. [5]
- **Clashmas quests**: seasonal quests with coins and carrots; all of
  them unlock the Spear Goblin skin "Candy Elf". [3] (texts:
  TID_…_CANDY_ELF, "Finish X Clashmas Quests…")
- **Season road / Mini Pass**: quests give carrots (Pony Fuel); free and
  paid lane; the free lane has an exclusive mini. Rewards not claimed by
  the season's end are lost. [4][5]
- Heroes and minis unlock with their first fragment, at scripted points of
  the progression rather than by random drops. [4]
- Heroes: 10 levels for gold, perks at 5 and 10, kept over seasons. Minis:
  3 stars that come with fragments automatically, without gold; the stars
  are lost at the season's end and their effects change each season. [4]
- Gold is scarce, upgrade costs rise steeply (a Countess upgrade 800
  gold). [4]
- All purchases had daily caps, the pass a seasonal cap. [4]
- Hero upgrades give player exp; level ups give small currency rewards. [4]

## Seasons and leagues

- Seasons were expected to last about two months (60 or 90 days). [4]
- At a season's start players get rewards by the stars of the previous
  one (season_rewards.csv: EndStars by star count). [4]
- Seasons known: Pre-Season 1 "The Adventure Begins" / "Welcome to Clash
  Mini!" (Countess, Monk; Healing Temple, Electro Valley, Banquet Hall),
  Season 2 "The Adventure Continues!" (texts in our APK), Season 3 from
  16 May (Skeleton King, Royal Ghost: not in our client). [6]

## From the Clash Mini Fandom wiki (pages saved by the user)

- **Mini Pass, Pre-Season 1 and Season 1 "The Adventure Begins"** (8 Nov
  2021, Countess and Monk, boards Healing Temple, Electro Valley, Banquet
  Hall): **90 reward tiers**, each **1–3 Pony Fuel**; rewards mini and hero
  fragments, Crystal Coins (Challenge Coins in Pre-Season 1), minis, gold,
  some gems; two exclusive skins: **Plush P.E.K.K.A at tier 26, Luchador
  Monk at tier 87**. Mini Pass 9.99 $, Mini Pass Plus 14.99 $ (only 10
  tiers ahead). It lasted 119 days (with the pre-season); Season 2
  (7 March 2022) 70 days, Season 3 63 days. The bonus chest after the last
  tier (2 hero fragments per 6 fuel) came with Season 2.
- **Crystal Coins** (21 Dec 2021, our version): replaced Challenge
  Coins; skins **Fabulous Prince 600, Crystal P.E.K.K.A 100** Crystal Coins;
  at most 9999. (Later: 800 per skin from March 2022.)
- **Quests**: added at launch (8 Nov 2021); **Challenge Quests** came on
  21 Dec 2021 (our version). The page's numbers (6 daily, weekly, career)
  are from 2023.
- Not in our client (later): the Login Calendar, Hero Shards, Toy Machine,
  Gallery.
- Gem packs (real money, not for us): 50 for 1.99 $ … 5000 for 99.99 $.

## From screenshots of our version (December 2021, sent by the user)

Shop (account level 13):
- **Clashmas Special** (timer 14 d 10 h): bundle "Clashmas Skins" (Gift
  Giant Skeleton, Sparkle Archer, Snowler; 7.99 €, 1 purchase, "+160%
  extra"); **Jolly Skin 159 gems**, **Helpful Skin 99 gems** (the Mini
  P.E.K.K.A's, our data's "Windup"). The gems after both buys fit:
  355 − 159 − 99 = 97.
- Starter Bundle (4.99 €, 1 purchase; content not visible).
- **Minis** (daily timer): 3 offers, **1 each, +1 fragment** for **250,
  500, 750 gold** (Skeleton Guard, Musketeer, Battle Healer).
- **Heroes** (same timer): 3 offers, **5 each**; before Clashmas
  **+25 fragments for 100 gems** (Countess, Monk, Shield Maiden).
- **Crystal Coin skins**: Crystal P.E.K.K.A 100, Fabulous Prince 600.
- Not in our version any more: the Challenge Coin module (free 5, 5 for
  150 gold, 5 for 15 gems; removed 21 Dec 2021).

Quests:
- **Clashmas Quests** (timer 15 d 1 h): "Finish 12 Clashmas Quests to
  unlock Candy Elf Skin!", three open at a time. Seen: win 10 trophies
  (100 gold), play 6 duels (1 Musketeer fragment), play 3 rumbles (1
  fragment), win 3:0 in a duel (x/3; 1–3 random fragments), use Prince to
  charge 3 enemies in a match (5 + 5 Crystal Coins, 10 gems), play 6
  matches with Royal Champion (5 + 5 + 5 Crystal Coins).
- **Daily quests: 6 a day** ("Quests completed: 0/6"), **1 reroll a day**.
  Each gives **1 Pony Fuel** (the golden heart) and gold: **10–30** (win 1
  match without rerolling, knock out 15 enemies) or **100** (win 9
  rounds).
- **Mini quests** below them ("Win 5 matches using …": 1 Pony Fuel and a
  fragment).
- The road's title: "The Adventure Begins".

## Later versions (not for us)

1.1589.3 (Season 3: Trophy Road, Raids, Supercell ID), July 2023 (no
moving units after placing them, Rumble draft, Magic Tiles), December 2023
"Clash Mini 2.0" (classes, cosmetic levels, Toy Machine loot boxes),
end of development announced March 2024. [6][7][8] From them we take numbers
only (amounts, prices, counts), never units, chests, texts or art our
client does not have.
